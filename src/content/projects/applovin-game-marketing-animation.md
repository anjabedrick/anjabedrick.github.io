<p class="pill pill-label">Orientation</p>
<h2>The challenge</h2>
<p class="sec-sub">Rapid ideation, animation, and iteration using A/B testing. Check out this storyboard I put together to convey a new concept to the team.</p>
<div class="star star-stack">
<div class="star-card" data-k="situation">
<h4>Situation</h4>
<p>Beginning as a motion design intern for AppLovin while working on my bachelor's, I ended up working part-time with AppLovin for two years.</p>
</div>
<div class="star-card" data-k="problem">
<h4>Problem</h4>
<p>Digital marketing for a diverse array of partners with equally diverse viewers means that successful campaigns must be extremely flexible and responsive to the changing interests of these varied viewer groups.</p>
</div>
<div class="star-card" data-k="action">
<h4>Action</h4>
<p>AppLovin's specialty is understanding and quickly adapting to data. If one month, hyper-casual word gamers click, install, and keep games that have been marketed as relaxing, the next month, they might respond to games marketed as mentally stimulating. We adapt ads to these signals as fast as they change.</p>
</div>
<div class="star-card" data-k="result">
<h4>Result</h4>
<p>Years of refining this process means that AppLovin is one of the top game advertising platforms in the world. I am still impressed by the efficiency with which we had to, and were able to, implement new data in real time. This efficient data-driven workflow is something I've brought with me into each new chapter.</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
