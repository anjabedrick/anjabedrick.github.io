<div class="star-card" data-k="situation">
<h4>Situation</h4>
<p>An exchange course in advanced graphic design using a real-world example. At the time, SNM was in the process of rebranding. What factors would the professional designers take into account?</p>
</div>
<div class="star-card" data-k="problem">
<h4>Problem</h4>
<p>Redesigning the Danish natural history museum's visual identity, complete with a design system, ad campaign mockups, and motion graphics.</p>
</div>
<div class="star-card" data-k="action">
<h4>Action</h4>
<p>Understanding the history and local cultural context of SNM, hand sketching concepts, designing and animating a holistic visual identity.</p>
</div>
<div class="star-card" data-k="result">
<h4>Result</h4>
<p>A visual identity guide complete with mockups</p>
</div>
