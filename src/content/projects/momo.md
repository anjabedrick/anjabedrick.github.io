## Challenge

<div class="star">
<div class="star-cell">
<h4>Situation</h4>
<p>A collaboration between interactions design and product design master&#x27;s programs with a focus on auditory feedback.</p>
</div>
<div class="star-cell">
<h4>Problem</h4>
<p>The need for accessible, quality fine-motor skill training physiotherapy is increasing globally. However, 70% of patients struggle to keep up with their practice at home. How might we design a home-based practice that patients want to engage with?</p>
</div>
<div class="star-cell">
<h4>Action</h4>
<p>Building on the experience of our two teammembers who are engaged in long-term physiotherapy, we prototyped quickly using 3D printed shells and Arduino electronics to iterate rapidly.</p>
</div>
<div class="star-cell">
<h4>Result</h4>
<p>A modular, gamified fine-motor-skill training tool that challenges, motivates, and adapts to each patient&#x27;s ability and goals.</p>
</div>
</div>

### 04 Modules

<p class="label">The brain, wrist rotation, finger strength, and dexterity are a few modules based on real fine motor skill tuning exercises that we brought to MoMo.</p>

## Output

MoMo is a modular physiotherapy tool that adjusts to suit patients' needs, rather than the other way around. Built on real tools and research, this prototype employs gamified light and sound feedback to address low treatment-adherence.
